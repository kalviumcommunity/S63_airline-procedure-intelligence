# Airline Procedure Intelligence (SkyOps Intelligence)

AI-powered operational procedure assistant for airline ground staff, ramp teams, station operations, and safety compliance personnel.

## Overview
A high-integrity Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform designed to deliver authoritative, verified, and timely operational procedures from Ground Operations Manuals (GOM), Safety Bulletins, Airport Handling Manuals (AHM), and Regulatory Directives (FAA, EASA, DGCA, ICAO).
