Ground Fuel Servicing with Passengers Embarking / Disembarking

⚠️
Mandatory Safety Warning (OSB-2026-04 Override)
Ground fueling during active passenger embarkation is permitted ONLY when designated aircraft exits remain completely unobstructed with passenger boarding bridges or ground stairs positioned at minimum two door exits.
Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
🦺 High-Visibility Reflective Vest 🥾 ANSI Steel-Toe Ramp Boots 🎧 Noise-Attenuating Ear Defenders 🧤 Nitrile Fuel-Resistant Gloves

Procedural Action Checklist:

1
The Fueling Supervisor MUST establish direct positive two-way communication with the Flight Deck crew and Senior Cabin Crew Member before connecting the hydrant pit coupler.
2
Main Cabin Door L1 MUST remain actively mated to the Jet Bridge or ground mobile stairs with exterior egress corridors completely clear of baggage carts and GSE.
3
An additional exit (Door L2 or R2) MUST have ground stairs deployed or an unimpeded evacuation slide clearance zone maintained free of fuel tankers or service trucks.
4
Ground crew SHALL NOT connect or disconnect Ground Power Units (GPU) or 400Hz umbilical cables while fueling nozzles are actively locked into fuel adapters.
5
If fuel vapor is detected inside the cabin or an apron spill occurs, fueling MUST BE IMMEDIATELY SUSPENDED via the Emergency Fuel Shut-off (EFSO) switch.
